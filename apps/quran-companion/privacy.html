Privacy Policy

Quran Companion · Last updated: 16 August 2026

Quran Companion (“the App”) is designed to help you read and listen to the Quran with as little data collection as possible. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the App.

1. Summary

  • No account is required to read the Quran.
  • Reading progress, bookmarks, private notes, plans, and settings stay on your device.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • Audio streaming and optional online features may contact network servers.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally (for example with on-device storage):

  • Last reading position and listening progress
  • Bookmarks
  • Private verse notes (never used for analytics or AI training)
  • Reading plan preferences
  • Language, theme, and reader display preferences
  • Onboarding completion flag

This information does not leave your device unless you later enable a sync feature (not part of the current MVP) or you export/share content yourself (for example Share or Copy).

3. Network use

When you play audio, the App streams recitation files from third-party content delivery networks. Those requests may include technical data typical of any internet connection (such as IP address and device user-agent) processed by the CDN provider under their own terms.

Bundled Quran text and translations are shipped inside the App and do not require a network connection to read.

4. Analytics and advertising

The current MVP does not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. Crash reporting, if added later, will be disclosed in an updated policy before shipping.

5. Children

The App is a general religious reading tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because reading data stays on-device for MVP, there is no server-side profile for minors.

6. Permissions

  • Internet — optional audio streaming
  • Foreground media service — background playback controls when you listen
  • Location (optional) — only if you choose “Use my location” for prayer times; coordinates stay on your device
  • Notifications (optional) — local prayer reminders you enable; no remote push server

Microphone access is not required for reading or playback.

7. Your choices

  • Clear App data / uninstall to remove local progress, bookmarks, and notes
  • Stay offline to avoid audio network requests

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
