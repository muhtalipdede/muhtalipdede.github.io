Metro Control: Istanbul Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026 (version 0.2.0)
App: Metro Control: Istanbul (com.muhtalipdede.metrocontrol)
Platforms: Google Play (Android); App Store (iOS) when published; web build for development
Developer: Muhtalip Dede
Contact: muhtalipdede@gmail.com

Metro Control: Istanbul is an offline metro dispatch puzzle. There are no Metro Control accounts and no Metro Control server: your progress stays on your device. From version 0.2.0 the Android app shows ads and offers one optional in-app purchase, described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Your best stars and score per level, your Endless Rush best score, retry counts (to offer hints), which rule cards you have seen, free-hint counters, the “remove ads” flag and your settings (language, music, sound, vibration, reduced motion, graphics) are saved in the app's local storage on your device. This data is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

3. Advertising (Android)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions, under Google's policies. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads; without consent only non-personalized ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (shown where required). Ads are rated for a general audience.

Rewarded videos are always your choice: you only see one when you tap “Watch ad” for a hint or a rewind. You get 3 free hints every day without ads. The Remove ads purchase turns off the ads between levels and gives hints and rewinds without videos.

4. In-app purchase (Android)

The purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. The developer does not receive your payment card details or your Google account information, only whether the purchase completed. Use Settings → Restore purchases after reinstalling to get Remove ads back.

5. Device features

The app may use your device's vibration motor for short haptic feedback. You can turn this off in Settings.

6. Google Play and Apple

Google Play and the App Store may collect information about installs, crashes and performance under their own policies. The developer only sees aggregated statistics and cannot identify individual players.

7. Children

Metro Control: Istanbul is a general-audience puzzle game that is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Retention and deletion

To delete your progress, use Settings → Reset progress, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. There is no account or server copy to delete. Data processed by Google for ads is kept under Google's retention policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

9. Not an official app

Station and line names are inspired by the real Istanbul network. Track layouts, switches and incidents are fictional, made for gameplay. This is not an official Metro İstanbul app and is not affiliated with any transport operator.

10. Changes

If a future version adds analytics, crash reporting, or ads and purchases on iOS, this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
