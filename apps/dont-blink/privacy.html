Don't Blink Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026
App: Don't Blink (com.muhtalipdede.dontblink)
Platforms: Google Play (Android) and App Store (iOS)
Contact: muhtalipdede@gmail.com

This policy describes how Don't Blink handles information. Don't Blink is a local-first reaction game. It does not operate user accounts and does not run a Don't Blink backend that receives your scores.

1. Summary

TopicPractice
AccountsNone
Backend / cloud syncNone (MVP)
Where data is storedOn your device (Android DataStore / iOS UserDefaults)
AdsAndroid: Google AdMob — an optional rewarded “second chance” and occasional interstitials between runs (never during a round, none in your first games). Removable with the one-time Remove ads purchase. iOS: no ads.
In-app purchasesAndroid: “Remove ads”, processed by Google Play Billing; Restore purchases in Settings
Analytics / crash SDKsNone
Sale of personal dataNo

2. Information processed

On-device only: best score, best streak, games played, tap counts, average reaction time, daily challenge result and daily streak, settings (haptics, sound), and a cached “remove ads” purchase flag.

If you use Share, the system share sheet (Android or iOS) may send an image and text you chose to another app (Messages, etc.) under that app's terms. Don't Blink does not upload that card to a Don't Blink server.

3. How information is used

Your scores and settings are used only to run the game on your device. Don't Blink does not sell data and does not upload your scores to a Don't Blink server.

4. Advertising (Android)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, and device and app information under the Google advertising policies. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), a Google consent form appears before any ad request; ads are personalized only with your consent. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

The rewarded ad is always your choice (“Second chance”). The Remove ads purchase turns off interstitials and makes second chances free.

5. In-app purchases (Android)

Purchases go through Google Play Billing. Don't Blink does not receive your payment details. Use Restore purchases in Settings after reinstalling.

6. Third parties

Google Play and Apple App Store: distribution and store privacy disclosures apply. Google AdMob and Google Play Billing (Android) as described above. No analytics, crash, or social SDKs.

7. Retention and deletion

Clear app storage or uninstall to remove Don't Blink data. Account deletion: not applicable — there are no accounts.

8. Children

Don't Blink is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

9. Changes

Updates to this policy will change the effective date and, when required, the store listing and in-app disclosure.
