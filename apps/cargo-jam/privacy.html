Cargo Jam Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026
App: Cargo Jam (com.muhtalipdede.cargojam)
Platforms: Google Play (Android) and App Store (iOS); web build for development
Developer: Muhtalip Dede
Contact: muhtalipdede@gmail.com

Cargo Jam is a local-first logistics puzzle. The core campaign works offline. There are no Cargo Jam accounts and no Cargo Jam backend that stores your progress.

2. Information processed on device

Level progress, stars, settings (sound, reduced motion), daily free hint counters, hint pack balance, cosmetic ownership, and purchase flags (for example “remove ads”) stay on your device.

3. Advertising

When ads are enabled, third-party ad networks (for example Google AdMob) may process device advertising identifiers and contextual signals under their own policies. Personalized ads and related tracking run only after required consent (for example GDPR consent forms and Apple App Tracking Transparency where applicable). You can remove between-level interstitial ads with the Remove ads purchase; rewarded hints remain optional.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases go through Google Play Billing or Apple StoreKit. Cargo Jam does not receive your full payment card details. Use Restore purchases in Settings if you reinstall.

5. Analytics

Consent-gated gameplay events (level start/complete, hint source, ad completion, purchase outcomes) may be sent to an analytics provider to improve difficulty and fairness. Events avoid names, emails, and other direct identifiers. If you decline analytics consent, these events are not sent.

5a. Crash reports

To find and fix bugs, the Android app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent with the error and stack trace, the level you were playing, the app and OS version, the device model, and a random Crashlytics installation identifier. Reports do not include your name, email or advertising ID and are not used for advertising. Google processes them as our service provider under the Firebase privacy terms and keeps them for up to 90 days. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Send crash reports.

6. Children

Cargo Jam is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Do not enable child-directed treatment without a separate compliance decision.

7. Retention and deletion

Clear app storage or uninstall to remove on-device data. There is no cloud account to delete.

8. Changes

Updates to this policy change the effective date and, when required, the store listing and in-app disclosure.
