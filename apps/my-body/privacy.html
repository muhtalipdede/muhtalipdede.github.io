My Body Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026
App: My Body (com.muhtalipdede.mybody)
Contact: muhtalipdede@gmail.com

This policy describes how My Body handles information on your device. My Body is a local-first measurement, clothing-size estimate, nutrition-habit, and home-training companion. It does not operate user accounts and does not run a My Body backend that receives your body or health data.

1. Summary

2. Information My Body processes

2.1 Body measurements (sensitive)

Optional height, weight, and tape circumferences you enter (for example abdomen, chest, hip) with dates and measurement conditions. Used for on-device metrics (such as BMI), illustrative 2D silhouettes, clothing size ranges, and progress views. Body-fat percentage is off by default and is not invented.

2.2 Nutrition and activity logs

Free-text meal notes, optional portion notes, manual walking duration/distance, and workout session logs (sets, RPE, discomfort flags). No food database upload; no Health Connect requirement in MVP.

2.3 Goals, preferences, reminders

Goals you approve, language/appearance/units preferences, and optional local notification schedules. Reminder text is designed not to show weight on the lock screen.

2.4 Exports you initiate

JSON/CSV or password-protected backups you create and share via the system share sheet. The app cannot remotely delete copies you already shared.

3. How information is used

Only to power on-device features: measurement history, metrics with caveats, clothing estimates from bundled charts, workouts, meal habits, weekly review suggestions (with your consent), and optional local reminders. No advertising use, no sale, no My Body server upload in MVP.

4. Third parties

Google Play: Distribution and Data Safety disclosures apply. MVP includes no separate analytics, ads, crash, or social SDKs.

Operating system: Share sheet, file picker, and notification APIs may process data under your device OS terms when you use those features.

5. Retention and deletion

  1. Settings → Delete all local data wipes SQLite personal data under app control and cancels scheduled local reminders.
  2. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove remaining app data.
  3. Device loss or reinstall without a backup you created will lose local data.

Account deletion: Not applicable — My Body has no accounts.

6. Children

My Body is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

7. Medical / wellness disclaimer

My Body is a wellness and self-tracking app. BMI and ratios are not diagnoses. Clothing sizes are chart-based estimates, not guarantees. Workouts are low-impact suggestions; stop if you feel unwell and seek professional care when appropriate. My Body does not provide medical advice or promised body transformations.

8. International / transfers

MVP processing is on-device. My Body does not transfer My Body-stored body data to a My Body cloud service.

9. Legal review

KVKK/GDPR counsel review is an external launch checklist item for the developer; this page describes actual MVP behavior and is not legal advice.

10. Changes

Updates to this policy will change the effective date and, when required, the Play listing and in-app disclosure.
