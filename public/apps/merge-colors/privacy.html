Merge Colors Privacy Policy

Effective: 29 August 2026
App: Merge Colors (com.muhtalipdede.mergecolors)
Developer: Muhtalip Dede
Contact: muhtalipdede@gmail.com

Merge Colors is a local-first puzzle game. No accounts. No Merge Colors backend. Best score, highest color, daily result, and settings stay on your device. Optional share uses the system share sheet only. No ads, analytics, or crash SDKs in the current version.

On-device data

  • Best score and highest color tier
  • Games played
  • Daily challenge date and today’s best
  • Settings (sound, haptics, colorblind patterns, hint seen)

This data does not leave your device unless you choose Share and send an image through another app.

We do not collect

No account signup, no ads, no analytics SDKs, no location or contacts for gameplay.

Deletion

Uninstall the app or clear app storage. There is no cloud account to delete.
