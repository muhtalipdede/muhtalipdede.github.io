Harbor Stack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026
App: Harbor Stack (com.muhtalipdede.harborstack)
Platforms: Google Play (Android); web build for development
Developer: Muhtalip Dede
Contact: muhtalipdede@gmail.com

Harbor Stack is an offline container puzzle. It has no accounts and no server of its own. The game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your unlocked levels, stars per level and the sound setting in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

3. Network access

All game content, fonts, sounds and levels are built into the app, and the game works fully offline. The Android app does not send any data over the network.

4. Google Play

Google Play may collect information about installs, crashes and device performance under its own policies (for example Android vitals). The developer only sees aggregated statistics in the Play Console and cannot identify individual players.

5. Children

Harbor Stack collects no personal data from anyone, including children. It is a general-audience puzzle game.

6. Retention and deletion

To delete your progress, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. There is no cloud account or server copy to delete.

7. Changes

If a future version adds ads, purchases, analytics or crash reporting, this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date. The store listing and in-app disclosures will be updated when required.
