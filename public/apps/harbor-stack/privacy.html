Harbor Stack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 (version 1.0.0)
App: Harbor Stack (com.muhtalipdede.harborstack)
Platforms: Google Play (Android) and App Store (iOS); web build for development
Developer: Muhtalip Dede
Contact: muhtalipdede@gmail.com

Harbor Stack is an offline container puzzle. There are no Harbor Stack accounts and no Harbor Stack server: your progress stays on your device. From version 1.0.0 the Android app shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases, described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Unlocked levels, stars, your daily puzzle streak, settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, graphics, language), free hint counters, your hint balance and purchase flags (for example “remove ads”) are saved in the app's local storage. This data is not sent to the developer.

3. Advertising (Android)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions, under Google's policies. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads; without consent only non-personalized ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (shown where required). Ads are rated for a general audience.

Rewarded videos are always your choice: you only see one when you tap “Watch video” for a hint. The Remove ads purchase turns off the ads between levels; rewarded videos stay available as an option.

4. In-app purchases (Android)

Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. The developer does not receive your payment card details or your Google account information, only whether a purchase completed. Use Settings → Restore purchases after reinstalling to get Remove ads back. Hint packs are consumable and are not restored.

5. Google Play and Apple

Google Play and the App Store may collect information about installs, crashes and performance under their own policies. The developer only sees aggregated statistics and cannot identify individual players.

6. Children

Harbor Stack is a general-audience puzzle game that is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Retention and deletion

To delete your progress, use Settings → Reset progress, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. There is no account or server copy to delete. Data processed by Google for ads is kept under Google's retention policies; you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

8. Usage statistics (Android)

The Android app uses Google Firebase Analytics to see how many players come back and how long play sessions last, so the game can be improved. It records app opens, session length and screens, together with the device model, operating system and app version, an approximate country (derived from the IP address, which is not stored) and a random app-instance ID. Analytics does not collect the advertising ID, and this data is not used to personalise ads. Google processes it under its privacy policy; event-level data is kept for 2 months. Clearing the app's storage resets the app-instance ID.

9. Changes

28 November 2026: Google Firebase Analytics added to the Android app (section 8).

If a future version adds crash reporting, or ads and purchases on iOS, this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
