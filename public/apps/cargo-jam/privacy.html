Cargo Jam Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026
App: Cargo Jam (com.muhtalipdede.cargojam)
Platforms: Google Play (Android) and App Store (iOS); web build for development
Developer: Muhtalip Dede
Contact: muhtalipdede@gmail.com

Cargo Jam is a local-first logistics puzzle. The core campaign works offline. There are no Cargo Jam accounts and no Cargo Jam backend that stores your progress. On Android you can optionally use Google Play Games for achievements and a cloud backup of your progress.

2. Information processed on device

Level progress, stars, settings (sound, reduced motion), daily free hint counters, hint pack balance, cosmetic ownership, and purchase flags (for example “remove ads”) stay on your device.

2a. Google Play Games (Android)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Cargo Jam uses it for two things: achievements, and a single Saved Games slot holding a copy of your progress (unlocked levels, stars, hint balance, cosmetics, daily streak and purchase flags such as remove ads) so it can be restored on a new device. Google Play Games shares your Play Games player ID and profile name with the game; Cargo Jam does not send them to any server of its own. The saved copy is stored by Google in your Google account under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without Play Games; you can remove the saved data and the game's access from the Google Play Games app settings.

3. Advertising

When ads are enabled, third-party ad networks (for example Google AdMob) may process device advertising identifiers and contextual signals under their own policies. Personalized ads and related tracking run only after required consent (for example GDPR consent forms and Apple App Tracking Transparency where applicable). You can remove between-level interstitial ads with the Remove ads purchase; rewarded hints remain optional.

On Android, Google AdMob also runs an auction among mediation partners so the best-paying ad is shown: Unity Ads (Unity Technologies, privacy policy) and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle, privacy policy). When they bid or show an ad, they may receive the same kind of data as AdMob (advertising ID, IP address, device and app information) under their own policies and your consent choices.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases go through Google Play Billing or Apple StoreKit. Cargo Jam does not receive your full payment card details. Use Restore purchases in Settings if you reinstall.

5. Analytics

The Android app uses Google Firebase Analytics to see how many players come back and how long play sessions last, so the game can be improved. It records app opens, session length and screens, together with the device model, operating system and app version, an approximate country (derived from the IP address, which is not stored) and a random app-instance ID. Analytics does not collect the advertising ID, and this data is not used to personalise ads. Google processes it under its privacy policy; event-level data is kept for 2 months. Clearing the app's storage resets the app-instance ID.

5a. Crash reports

To find and fix bugs, the Android app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent with the error and stack trace, the level you were playing, the app and OS version, the device model, and a random Crashlytics installation identifier. Reports do not include your name, email or advertising ID and are not used for advertising. Google processes them as our service provider under the Firebase privacy terms and keeps them for up to 90 days. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Send crash reports.

6. Children

Cargo Jam is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Do not enable child-directed treatment without a separate compliance decision.

7. Retention and deletion

Clear app storage or uninstall to remove on-device data. There is no Cargo Jam account to delete. To remove the Play Games cloud copy and achievements, delete Cargo Jam data from your Google Play Games profile settings.
