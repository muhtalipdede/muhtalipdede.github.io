Metro Control: Istanbul is an offline metro dispatch puzzle. It has no accounts and no server of its own. The game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your best stars and score per level, how many times you retried a level (to offer hints), which rule cards you have seen, and your settings (language, sound, vibration, reduced motion) in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

3. Device features

The app may use your device's vibration motor for short haptic feedback during play. You can turn this off in the game's settings. No other device sensors or permissions are used.

4. Network access

All game content, levels, images, fonts and sounds are built into the app, and the game works fully offline. The mobile builds do not send any game data over the network.

5. Google Play / App Store

Google Play and Apple may collect information about installs, crashes and device performance under their own policies. The developer only sees aggregated statistics in the store consoles and cannot identify individual players.

6. Children

Metro Control: Istanbul collects no personal data from anyone, including children. It is a general-audience puzzle game.

7. Retention and deletion

To delete your progress, use "Reset progress" in the game's settings, clear the app's storage in device settings, or uninstall the app. There is no cloud account or server copy to delete.

8. Not an official app

Station and line names are inspired by the real Istanbul network. Track layouts, switches and incidents are fictional, made for gameplay. This is not an official Metro İstanbul app and is not affiliated with any transport operator.

9. Changes

If a future version adds ads, purchases, analytics or crash reporting, this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date. The store listing and in-app disclosures will be updated when required.
