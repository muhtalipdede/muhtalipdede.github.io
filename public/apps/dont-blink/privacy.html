Don't Blink Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2026
App: Don't Blink (com.muhtalipdede.dontblink)
Contact: muhtalipdede@gmail.com

This policy describes how Don't Blink handles information. Don't Blink is a local-first reaction game. It does not operate user accounts and does not run a Don't Blink backend that receives your scores.

1. Summary

TopicPractice
AccountsNone
Backend / cloud syncNone (MVP)
Where data is storedOn your device (DataStore)
Analytics / ads / crash SDKsNone in MVP
Sale of personal dataNo

2. Information processed

On-device only: best score, best streak, games played, tap counts, average reaction time, daily challenge result for the current local date, and settings (haptics, sound).

If you use Share, Android's share sheet may send an image and text you chose to another app (Messages, etc.) under that app's terms. Don't Blink does not upload that card to a Don't Blink server.

3. How information is used

Only to run the game on your device. No advertising use, no sale, no Don't Blink-operated server upload in MVP.

4. Third parties

Google Play: Distribution and Data Safety disclosures apply. MVP includes no separate analytics, ads, crash, or social SDKs.

5. Retention and deletion

Clear app storage or uninstall to remove Don't Blink data. Account deletion: not applicable — there are no accounts.

6. Children

Don't Blink is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

7. Changes

Updates to this policy will change the effective date and, when required, the Play listing and in-app disclosure.
